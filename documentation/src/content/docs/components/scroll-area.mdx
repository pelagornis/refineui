# Scroll Area

Scroll Area is an overflow container with a thin custom scrollbar. Use it when a panel has a finite height and content that may grow past it — lists, long copy, or a nested table.

The viewport still scrolls with wheel, trackpad, and keyboard. The native bar is hidden; you compose a track and thumb. For split panes that change size, use **[Resizable](/components/resizable/)**. For a wide table, wrap **[Table](/components/table/)** in a fixed-height Scroll Area when you also need vertical overflow.

## Interface

### ScrollArea

| Prop | Default | Type |
|------|---------|------|
| `type` | `"hover"` | `"hover"` \| `"always"` — Show the scrollbar on hover, focus, or scroll, or always when overflow exists. |

Also accepts native `div` attributes (`className`, `style`, `aria-*`).

### ScrollAreaScrollbar

| Prop | Default | Type |
|------|---------|------|
| `orientation` | `"vertical"` | `"vertical"` \| `"horizontal"` — Scroll axis. |

Also accepts native `div` attributes.

### Parts

| Part | Role |
|------|------|
| `ScrollAreaViewport` | Scrollable region. Native overflow; native bar hidden. Focusable. |
| `ScrollAreaScrollbar` | Track. Click jumps the thumb. |
| `ScrollAreaThumb` | Draggable thumb sized to the visible ratio. |
